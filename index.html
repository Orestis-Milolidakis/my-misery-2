About Me

Hello! I am a fourth-year Ph.D. candidate in Mathematics at Example University, advised by Prof. Jordan Lee. My work focuses on the intersection of algebraic geometry and number theory, particularly on rational points on varieties and arithmetic dynamics.

Before coming here, I completed my M.Sc. in Pure Mathematics at Sample State University and my B.Sc. in Mathematics at College Town University. You can find my papers on the Research page and my full background on my CV.

Research Interests

  • Algebraic Number Theory and L-functions
  • Arithmetic Dynamics on Projective Varieties
  • Computational Algebra and SageMath / Macaulay2
