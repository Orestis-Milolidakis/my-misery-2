About Me

Hello! I am a miserable second-year Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at the National University of Singapore. I am unfortunate to be advised by Prof. Frank Stephan. My work focuses on the intersection of life being misery and existence being torment, with a touch on agony. According to the LLM that built this website, it also focuses on algebraic geometry and number theory, particularly on rational points on varieties and arithmetic dynamics. Just kidding, my work focuses on anything that will make a professor work with me. I have periodically been interested in Structural graph theory, algorithmic randomness, and meta-complexity.

Before coming here, I completed the interdiscilipinary M.Sc. program "Algorithms, Logic, and Discrete Mathematics", jointly offered by the math department and the computer science department of the National University of Athens, along with the Electrical and Computer engineering and the School of Applied Mathematical and Physical Sciences of the National Technical University of Athens. I completed my B.Sc. in Computer Science at the National University of Athens. You can find my papers on the Research page and my full background on my CV.
